Rules reference · Playtest 0.1

Core character

  • Attributes: Might, Skill, Vitality, Fortune.
  • Maximum HP = 10 + 2 × Vitality.
  • Levels 1–10. Level-ups grant attribute points and talents (details pending consolidation from the original rules).
  • Permanent death: when HP reaches zero, the character dies. Record the campaign; no resurrection in the core rules.

Combat

  • Both sides resolve attacks simultaneously.
  • Attack uses Skill plus weapon accuracy against Defence.
  • Defence = 4 + floor(Skill ÷ 2) + bonuses.
  • Damage = weapon damage + floor(Might ÷ 2).
  • Armour reduces damage by a flat amount; minimum damage on a successful hit is 1.
  • Natural 1 misses. Natural 6 is a critical hit (+2 damage).
  • Combat is intended to be brief, with several rolls and meaningful risk.
  • Fleeing: roll 4–6 to escape; failure exposes you to a final attack. Bosses cannot be fled from.

Exploration & progression

  • Four fixed regions; enemies do not scale with the character, so revisiting weaker areas is viable.
  • Each region has up to five encounters and an optional boss.
  • Rewards are mixed: XP, gold and a d6-based loot roll, with item rarity and modifiers.
  • HP persists between encounters. Healing is limited.

Equipment & crafting

  • Deep equipment progression: item rarity, modifiers, upgrades, crafting and material management are core design pillars.
  • Inventory entries in this prototype are manually tracked; full item catalogues and crafting rules will be added in later iterations.

This is an initial app prototype, not yet a complete reproduction of every table from the original rules document. Rules marked as pending should be reconciled with the full source before play.
